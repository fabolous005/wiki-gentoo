This article is **archived (obsolete)**. Contents are surely incorrect for current usage, and are intended for historical reference only.

TLDR:

**Do not use this article!**

Instead of waiting for a Gentoo Developer to pick up your ebuild and commit it to portage tree, you could speed up this procedure by proxy maintaining it. When you feel ready to do that, you may seek for a Gentoo developer to cooperate with either on [#gentoo-sunrise](irc://chat.freenode.net/#gentoo-sunrise) or by contacting the teams where your ebuild belongs too.

If you find one, you should provide him a complete [metadata.xml](http://www.gentoo.org/proj/en/devrel/handbook/handbook.xml?part=2&chap=4) file like the following one:
