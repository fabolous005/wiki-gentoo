# LinuxDays 2016+Gentoo Miniconf

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

On Saturday 8 October 2016 and Sunday 9 October 2016, the [LinuxDays 2016](https://www.linuxdays.cz/2016/) and the [Gentoo Miniconf](https://blogs.gentoo.org/miniconf-2016) will take place in Prague, Czech Republic.

This page is intended to collect public information about our presence and help organizing stuff.

## Gentoo booth

The Gentoo table is located in the [booth area, position C](https://www.linuxdays.cz/2016/en/venue/).

The following is an hour-by-hour schedule of who is present at the stand.

## Gentoo talks

There will be a Gentoo track at the conference, on Saturday in Room 111.
