# /etc/portage/make.profile

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

The **/etc/portage/make.profile/** or **/etc/make.profile/** directories are usually symlinked to the correct profile in [/var/db/repos/gentoo/profiles](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki//var/db/repos/gentoo/profiles).

For more detailed information see the [Profile (Portage)](<https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Profile_(Portage)>) article and the /etc/portage/make.profile related sections in man 5 portage.
