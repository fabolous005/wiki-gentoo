The [Sunrise project repository sources](https://gitweb.gentoo.org/proj/sunrise.git/) are available for review by the Gentoo community. The repository still holds a few packages that are not in Gentoo. However, please note that those packages are most likely very outdated may not work at all. Existing ebuilds mostly serve as a base for users who wish to take them over and maintain their own versions.
