## News

#### Mar 27, 2012: Applications are now open

Student applications are now open. We're looking forward to seeing your applications posted to our [GSoC page](http://www.google-melange.com/gsoc/org/google/gsoc2012/gentoo).

#### Feb 7, 2012: Google announces GSoC 2012

Google has [announced](http://google-opensource.blogspot.com/2012/02/google-summer-of-code-2012-is-on.html) the launch of the 2012 edition of the Summer of Code on its open source blog.

The next step for us will be working on our application and ideas.



## Important events

- March 9, 2300 UTC: **Organization application deadline**
- March 26: **Student application period begins**
- April 6, 1900 UTC: **Student application deadline**
- May 21: **Start of coding**
- July 13: **Midterm evaluation deadline**
- August 24: **Final evaluation deadline**

For more information, consult the [official timeline](http://google-melange.appspot.com/document/show/gsoc_program/google/gsoc2012/faqs#timeline).
