# Embedded systems

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

The following pages are related to the official Gentoo [Embedded Project](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Project:Embedded):

- [ARM hardware list](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/ARM_hardware_list) — a list of **ARM hardware** owned by people in the [#gentoo-embedded](ircs://irc.libera.chat/#gentoo-embedded) ([webchat](https://web.libera.chat/#gentoo-embedded)) and [#gentoo-arm](ircs://irc.libera.chat/#gentoo-arm) ([webchat](https://web.libera.chat/#gentoo-arm)) channels have running Gentoo.
- [RISC-V hardware list](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/RISC-V_hardware_list) — a list of **RISC-V hardware** owned by the Gentoo community within the [#gentoo-riscv](ircs://irc.libera.chat/#gentoo-riscv) ([webchat](https://web.libera.chat/#gentoo-riscv)) channel.
