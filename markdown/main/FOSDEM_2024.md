# FOSDEM 2024

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

On 2024-02-03 and 2024-02-04, the Free and Open Source Software Developers' European Meeting [FOSDEM 2024](https://fosdem.org/2024/) will take place in Brussels, Belgium.

Naturally a Gentoo crowd will be there. Unfortunately, however, this year **Gentoo Linux has not been granted a stand**.
Also, sadly, for another year there will be no official Beer Event.
