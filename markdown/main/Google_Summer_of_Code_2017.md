## Getting help

In case your question was not answered on our website, please do not hesitate to get in touch with us. Here is how you can reach us:

- [#gentoo-soc](ircs://irc.libera.chat/#gentoo-soc) ([webchat](https://web.libera.chat/#gentoo-soc)) IRC channel - (please be patient, there is not always someone on who can help)
- [gentoo-soc@lists.gentoo.org](mailto:gentoo-soc@lists.gentoo.org) mailing list - ([subscription required](https://www.gentoo.org/get-involved/mailing-lists/instructions.html), [Browse the archives](https://archives.gentoo.org/gentoo-soc/))
