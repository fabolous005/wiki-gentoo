# OpenRheinRuhr 2016

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

On Saturday 5 November 2016 and Sunday 6 November 2016, the [OpenRheinRuhr 2016](https://www.openrheinruhr.de/) will take place in Oberhausen, Germany.

This page is intended to collect public information about our presence and help organizing stuff.

## Gentoo booth

The Gentoo booth has been confirmed.

None planned.

The [call for papers](https://www.openrheinruhr.de/callforpapers.html) was open until 23 September 2016.
