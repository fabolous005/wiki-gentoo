# PPC

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

This article is a landing page for those interested in the **ppc** and **ppc64** architectures.

PPC and PPC64 historically includes [Power Macintosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Macintosh) computers and, in modern times, mostly consists of machines running [IBM Power microprocessors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Power_microprocessors) on a [Power ISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_ISA). Power ISAS presently developed by the [OpenPOWER Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenPOWER_Foundation).
