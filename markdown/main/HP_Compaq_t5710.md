# HP Compaq t5710

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

The HP Compaq t5710 is a thin client computer from 2005 with a Transmeta Efficeon TM8000 processor running at 1.2 Ghz and 250 Mb RAM.
