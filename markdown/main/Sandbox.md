# Sandbox

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

**Sandbox** could have a variety of meanings:

- [Gentoo Wiki:Sandbox](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Gentoo_Wiki:Sandbox) - A "public" Gentoo Wiki testing area.
- [Sandbox (Portage)](<https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Sandbox_(Portage)>) - A Portage tool for Gentoo development, and associated [project](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Project:Sandbox).
- [Simple sandbox](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Simple_sandbox) article - Virtual containers to secure applications.
