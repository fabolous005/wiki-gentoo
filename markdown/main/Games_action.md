## Chromium B.S.U.

![](https://wiki.gentoo.org/images/thumb/7/7e/Chromium-bsu_screenshot1.jpg/150px-Chromium-bsu_screenshot1.jpg)

[Chromium B.S.U.](http://chromium-bsu.sourceforge.net) is a classic arcade raster graphics vertical scrolling shoot 'em up single player game. It was written with games like [Raptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raptor:_Call_of_the_Shadows) in mind and the idea to enhance them for modern graphics. As such it uses opengl via [media-libs/freeglut](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/media-libs/freeglut) or alternatively sdl, supports fullscreen and modern audio themes. View a YouTube demo [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zzzM8tQgjmw).

`root #``emerge --ask games-action/chromium-bsu`
## Supertuxkart

![](https://wiki.gentoo.org/images/thumb/3/3d/Supertuxkart_screenshot1.jpg/150px-Supertuxkart_screenshot1.jpg)

Supertuxkart is a free kart racing game starring Tux. It's a fork of TuxKart and under active development. It features single-player, multi-player and a story mode. You can choose from a lot of funny [characters](https://supertuxkart.sourceforge.net/Characters) including Tux, Mozilla, Pidgin, Puffy and of course the GNU. There is also an addon manager integrated, you can find some addons [here](https://stkaddons.net). The gameplay combines regular kart racing with the possibility to collect items on the tracks with different properties (such as a cake bomb or a glue-like stuff to slow down opponents). You also have to look out for fuel items you can use to boost your speed for a short period of time. This makes the gameplay much more fun and reminds a bit of [Super Mario Kart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Mario_Kart). View a Vimeo demo [here](https://vimeo.com/55324161).

`root #``emerge --ask games-action/supertuxkart`
