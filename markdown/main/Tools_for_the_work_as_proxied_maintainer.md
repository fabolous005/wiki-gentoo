[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

### Document your Gentoo knowledge

[app-emacs/org-mode](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/app-emacs/org-mode) or [x11-misc/zim](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/x11-misc/zim) will help you to accumulate your knowledge fast and find everything quickly later.

### Compare and merge ebuilds (diff)

GUIs like [dev-util/meld](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/dev-util/meld) and command line tools like [app-misc/colordiff](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/app-misc/colordiff), [dev-util/imediff](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/dev-util/imediff) or [dev-util/riffdiff::guru](https://github.com/gentoo-mirror/guru/tree/master/dev-util/riffdiff) help to compare ebuilds.

### Testing ebuilds

Every proxied maintainer has to [test packages](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Package_testing) before committing them. A [test environment](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Test_environment) can be useful.
