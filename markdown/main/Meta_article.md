# Meta article

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

A **meta article** is an article that provides a general overview or summary of conceptually similar ideas and related software available in Gentoo Linux.

## Meta article categories

A [Meta article category](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Category:Meta) is available for tagging meta-style articles.
