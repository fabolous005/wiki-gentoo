# Meta (disambiguation)

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

Within the Gentoo project space, the term of 'meta' is often used as an adjective or a prefix. It has different meanings depending on the word it augments.

Meanings include:

- [Meta article](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Meta_article) — an article that provides a general overview or summary of conceptually similar ideas and related software available in Gentoo Linux.
- [Meta ebuilds](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Meta_ebuilds) — an ebuild containing a developer-selected, curated software suite that generally relates to a certain *family* of related software packages.
- [Metadistribution](https://wiki.gentoo.org/index.php?title=Metadistribution&action=edit&redlink=1)
- Metastructure - See the [Social Contract](https://www.gentoo.org/get-started/philosophy/social-contract.html) and [Metastructure Project](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Project:Metastructure).
