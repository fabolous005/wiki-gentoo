# Abbreviations, terminology, and jargon

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)

In order to aid new community members and harden concepts for existing ones, this article aims to serve as a location to collect terms, definitions, acronyms, colloquialisms, expressions, and all other types of jargon known to the Gentoo community.

As with any unique distribution with a storied history, specific colloquialisms such as "Portage tree", or deprecated terminology such as "Herds" have influenced conversation in the Gentoo community. These are the types of community-specific terminology that this article will try to document.

## Terminology and jargon

According to Wikipedia, [jargon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/jargon) is the specialized terminology associated with a particular field or area of activity. In Gentoo, this means terms such as [Portage](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Portage), [ebuild repository](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Ebuild_repository), [Profiles](<https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Profile_(Portage)>), and others that are not quickly or easily understood by [etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/etymology) alone.

- Ebuild repository
- [Ebuild repositories](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Ebuild_repository) are file-structures that can provide packages for installation on a Gentoo system. Sometimes shortened just to "repository" or even "repo". Not to be confused with the *Gentoo ebuild repository* (see below) which is the specific official ebuild repository that provides packages for Gentoo.

- emerge
- The primary [command](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Emerge) for the [Portage](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Portage) package manager. In Gentoo, "to emerge" means "to install".

- dist kernel
- dk
- Used when discussing Gentoo's prebuilt distribution kernel [sys-kernel/gentoo-kernel](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/sys-kernel/gentoo-kernel) or [sys-kernel/gentoo-kernel-bin](https://packages.gentoo.org/packages/sys-kernel/gentoo-kernel-bin).


- Gentoo ebuild repository
- Gentoo repository
- Gentoo repo
- The [Gentoo ebuild repository](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Ebuild_repository#The_Gentoo_ebuild_repository) is Gentoo's official, primary [ebuild repository](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Ebuild_repository) containing elements of the [EAPI](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/EAPI) used to install packages via the package manager. Sometimes colloquially referred to as *::gentoo*.

- gentoo.git
- [https://gitweb.gentoo.org/repo/gentoo.git](https://gitweb.gentoo.org/repo/gentoo.git) - the [git](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Git) repository that is used to maintain the Gentoo ebuild repository.

- [Meta ebuild](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Meta_ebuilds)
- Meta package
- An ebuild containing a developer-selected, curated software suite.

- p.use
- p.a\_k
- p.mask
- Shorthand for when writing /etc/portage/package.\* filenames. See [/etc/portage/package.use](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki//etc/portage/package.use), [/etc/portage/package.accept\_keywords](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki//etc/portage/package.accept_keywords), and [/etc/portage/package.mask](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki//etc/portage/package.mask).

- [Portage](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Portage)
- The package management framework for Gentoo. It downloads, unpacks, compiles, and installs packages.

- [Tinderbox](https://zwiebeltoralf.de/tinderbox.html)
- A build bot to identify build and installation issues of Gentoo Linux software packages.

- Upstream
- In the context of Gentoo, usually refers to the projects that create and maintain the software that is available through the Gentoo repository. If asked to "report upstream", refers to using the version control software or bug reporting services provided by the people who manage the original project.

## Deprecated terminology

These terms were once more common, but because of the evolution of Gentoo over the years they are now rarely used or no longer applicable. They are listed here for historical reasons and because they may still be encountered in content that has not been kept up to date.

- [Genkernel](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Genkernel)
- gk
- A tool created by Gentoo used to automate the build process of the kernel and initramfs. Deprecated. Using [a Distribution Kernel](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Project:Distribution_Kernel) is preferred.

- Gentoo tree
- Portage tree
- rsync tree
- "the tree"
- The historic title of the official, primary ebuild repository for Gentoo. Now more of a colloquial reference.

- Herd
- A deprecated term used to define a group of developers who shared maintenance responsibility for a specific group of [ebuilds](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Ebuild). Herds are now known as projects (see [the full list](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Project:Gentoo)).
