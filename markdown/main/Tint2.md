# tint2

From Gentoo Wiki

[Jump to:navigation](https://wiki.gentoo.org#mw-head)

[Jump to:search](https://wiki.gentoo.org#searchInput)


**tint2** is a lightweight panel/taskbar specifically made for [Openbox](https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/Openbox), but it can also work with other window managers.
